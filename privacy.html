Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

RJP Port ("the app") is a personal portfolio and digital business card for Roody Pointjour. This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

Data Collection

RJP Port does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no user accounts, no sign-up or sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind built into the app.

Clipboard

The Contact screen lets you copy the developer's email address to your device's clipboard. This happens entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere.

External Links

The app contains links to external sites (LinkedIn, GitHub, YouTube, and a downloadable resume PDF) which open in your device's browser. These sites are operated by third parties and are covered by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Children's Privacy

RJP Port does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to rpointjour@yahoo.com.
